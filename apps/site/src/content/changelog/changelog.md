# Changelog

## Mac app
### 1.0.0 — in development
- **Voice scrolling** - Whole-script tracking with backward scrolling, on-device, 60+ languages.
- **Invisible overlay** - Excluded from screen sharing and recordings; always on top, even over full-screen apps.
- **Menu bar workflow** - Start from clipboard, recent scripts, quick settings.
- **Sound and constant modes**, **presets**, **stats**, **audio input selection**, **follow cursor**, **hover to pause**, **live edit**.
- **Free tier** - 3 custom scripts plus a demo; Pro and Lifetime unlock unlimited.

## Web app
### 2026-11 (reliability update)

- **65 languages** (was 34), 51 detected automatically, including right-to-left layout for Arabic, Hebrew, Persian and Urdu and regional variants such as Brazilian Portuguese.
- **Voice commands** understand "go forward", "go restart" and "go to the end", wait a beat so a phrase inside a sentence is not taken as a command, and confirm on screen.
- **Voice scrolling** keeps listening through long pauses, recovers from engine restarts, explains offline and no-microphone cases, and keeps the screen awake.
- **Recording** shows elapsed time, survives a missing microphone or unplugged camera, and always releases the camera when you leave.
- **Mirror mode** flips on either axis.

### 2026-11 (launch)
- **Voice, sound and constant scrolling** in the browser, 34 languages auto-detected.
- **Mirror mode, video recording, Google Docs import, offline PWA.**
